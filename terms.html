2. What the game is

  • “Odlot” is a free logic game with arrows: levels, Rush mode, the daily challenge, missions, daily rewards and events.
  • The game has no user accounts. Progress, coins and settings are stored only on your phone.
  • The game shows ads (Google AdMob). Rewarded ads are always your choice.
  • The game adapts its difficulty to how you play. The content of hidden “?” tiles is decided at the moment they are revealed. In some levels and in Rush mode the ending can be harder and may require a booster (e.g. the “Hammer”). You don’t have to pay or watch ads for it: you can also get a booster with coins earned in the game, or simply play again. Details: section 4b of the privacy policy (section 10 of these terms).
  • The game may have a daily play limit (energy) that renews at midnight. When it runs out, you can keep playing after a rewarded ad, with coins, or after buying “Play without limit”.
  • Leaderboards with other players are run by Google Play Games. Your scores are sent there only if you use Play Games on your phone.
  • The game is intended for adults (18+).

3. Technical requirements

  • A phone or tablet with Android 8.0 or newer and the Google Play Store app.
  • The game itself does not need the internet. Purchases and restoring them, ads (including rewarded ads), leaderboards and downloading game settings do.
  • Purchases need a Google account with a payment method in Google Play.
