Privacy Policy for the game “Odlot”

Version: 6 November 2026

Wersja polska: https://echo-signal.github.io/odlot-game-docs/polityka-prywatnosci.html

1. Who is responsible

The controller is echo signal, based in Poland, e-mail: support@echosignal.dev (“we”). The game has no user accounts and no server of our own. The game never asks for your name, e-mail, phone number or photos.

2. Summary

  • Progress, settings and play history are stored only on your phone.
  • The game adapts its difficulty to how you play. The content of hidden “?” tiles is decided at the moment they are revealed.
  • Ads are served by Google AdMob. Personalised ads only with your consent (consent form at start in the EEA, UK and Switzerland).
  • Purchases are handled by Google Play. We never see your card details.
  • Game statistics and crash reports are collected by Google Firebase; in the EU/EEA, UK and Switzerland statistics with identifiers only with your consent.
  • Leaderboards with other players are run by Google Play Games: if you are signed in to Play Games, your Rush scores and farthest levels are sent there under your Play Games profile. You never give the game a nickname or an email address.
  • The game is intended for adults (18+).

3. What data we process

a) Data kept only on your device (not sent to us; deleted when you uninstall the game or clear its data):

  • progress: level, Rush record, coins, unlocked worlds and cosmetics, tutorials, daily rewards and missions;
  • your records: your five best Rush games with their dates, the farthest level won in the hard mode, the longest daily streak;
  • settings: sound, music, vibration, “fewer effects”, world choice;
  • daily play limit: how much play energy is left today and how many ads you watched for it;
  • purchases: whether you bought “Remove ads” or “Unlimited play”, and the IDs of purchases already credited (so coins are never added twice);
  • play history: install date and last visit, number of sessions, average session length, number of games, recent losses;
  • device information: phone model, amount of RAM, Android version;
  • country: from the SIM card or, without one, from the phone’s language settings (a country code only, not your location);
  • ads: estimated revenue of recent ad impressions (reported by AdMob), number of rewarded-ad offers shown and accepted;
  • your ad consent status (stored by the consent form using the IAB TCF standard);
  • the Google Play age signal (section 9), where Google provides it.

If Android backup is on, the system may save this data in your own backup in your Google account. We cannot access it.

b) Data collected by Google services used in the game (see section 5):

  • AdMob: IP address (which may be used to estimate approximate location, e.g. country or city), Android Advertising ID, App Set ID, interactions with ads and the app (e.g. launches, taps, video views), diagnostic information (e.g. launch time);
  • Google Play Billing: purchase data (product, price, order ID); payment data is collected only by Google;
  • Google Analytics for Firebase: app instance ID, advertising ID, device model, OS version, language, approximate location derived from the IP address, game version, sessions and in-game events (e.g. level completed, Rush score, booster used, rewarded ad watched, purchase), and once per session the set of features used to adapt the game (section 4b) together with the computed value;
  • Firebase Crashlytics: stack trace, app and device state at the time of the crash, a random installation ID;
  • Firebase Remote Config: country code, language, time zone, OS version – to fetch game settings.
  • Google Play Games Services (only if you use Play Games on your phone): your Play Games profile (player ID, gamer name and avatar, which you choose in Play Games), the scores the game sends to the leaderboards (Rush score, farthest level, farthest level in the hard mode), and analytics and diagnostic data Google collects to keep the service working. Who can see your scores depends on the visibility you set in your Play Games profile.

c) Downloading game settings. The game may download a file with new settings or a new version of the model from section 4b from an address given in the game’s remote configuration. The server hosting it sees your IP address in its logs, like any website. We send no data about you there.

d) Messages you send us. If you write to support@echosignal.dev (e.g. a complaint, a withdrawal from a purchase or a question), we process your e-mail address, the content of the message and what you include in it (e.g. a Google Play order number).

4b. How the game adapts (profiling). A model running on your phone estimates, at the start of every session, how much ad revenue the game may earn over the next 7 days. It uses the data from 3a: phone model and memory, Android version, country, settings, play history, revenue from ads shown, and ad consent. Based on this, the game plans the session, e.g.:

  • how easy or hard the next levels and moments in Rush mode are, how many revives you get, how often a full-screen ad appears between levels, and when you get a rewarded-ad offer;
  • which arrow turns out to be under a hidden “?” tile – hidden tiles are not fixed in advance; the game chooses their content when they are revealed, depending on how the game is going;
  • in some levels and in Rush mode the ending can be harder and may require a booster (e.g. the “Hammer”). You don’t have to watch ads: you can get a booster by watching a rewarded ad or with coins earned just by playing (completed levels, daily rewards, missions), or simply play again. Harder endings never appear in the first levels, are limited per session and never apply to users flagged as minors.

This profiling has no legal or similarly significant effects on you (it only affects gameplay). The model runs on your phone; its inputs and result are also sent to Google Analytics, where they are used for statistics and to train future versions of the model.

6. Retention

  • Data on your phone: until you uninstall the game or clear its data.
  • Your messages to us: as long as needed to handle the matter, and then until claims related to it become time-barred.
  • Firebase Analytics: user-level data for 2 months; aggregated reports longer.
  • Firebase Crashlytics: 90 days.
  • AdMob and Google Play: according to Google’s policies (links in section 5).
  • Google Play Games: until you delete the game’s data or your profile in Play Games (https://play.google.com/games/profile).

7. Security

Data sent over the internet is encrypted (HTTPS). Game data on your phone is stored in the app’s private storage, which other apps cannot read.

8. Your rights

You have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, to object (including to the profiling in 4b), to withdraw consent at any time (without affecting earlier processing), and to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority – in Poland the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (UODO, ul. Stawki 2, 00-193 Warsaw), or the authority in your country.

How:

  • consent to ads and statistics: in the game, Settings → “Ad privacy settings” (in the EEA, UK and Switzerland) – the change applies at once; you can also clear the game’s data (Android Settings → Apps → Odlot → Storage) and the form will appear again;
  • advertising ID: Android Settings → Google (or Privacy) → Ads → “Delete advertising ID”;
  • on-device data and game profile: clear the game’s data or uninstall it;
  • other rights: e-mail support@echosignal.dev. As you have no account with us, we may ask for information that lets us find your data (e.g. the app instance ID).

9. Children and the age signal

The game is intended for adults (18+) and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect children’s data. If you believe a child has used the game, contact us and we will delete the data we can access.

Where Google Play provides an age signal (Play Age Signals API, currently e.g. in Brazil and some US states), the game checks the account’s age range once a day. If it indicates a minor, the game does not use deliberately harder endings, does not show rewarded-ad offers at the predicted end of a session, and marks ad requests as under the age of consent with ad content limited to the “T” (teen) rating. The age signal stays on the phone; we do not send it to analytics and do not use it for ad personalisation or profiling.

10. Residents of US states with privacy laws

If you live in a US state with a consumer privacy law (e.g. California, Virginia, Colorado, Connecticut, Texas), you have additional rights:

  • opt out of the “sale” or “sharing” of personal information and of targeted advertising: in the game, Settings → “Ad privacy settings” → choose to opt out. After that, AdMob shows only non-personalised ads. We do not sell personal information for money, but showing personalised ads through AdMob may count as “sharing” or targeted advertising under these laws;
  • access, correct and delete your data: e-mail support@echosignal.dev. Using these rights does not change how you can use the game.

We do not knowingly sell or share personal information of consumers under 16.

11. Changes

We will announce material changes in this policy (date at the top) and in the game’s update notes.
